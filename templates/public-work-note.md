## Publication check

- [ ] No customer, personal, credential, infrastructure, recovery or private repository information
- [ ] No unresolved security finding
- [ ] No unsupported price, metric, timeline or future capability
- [ ] Contribution claim is backed by an exact public link that the reviewer opened and checked
- [ ] Changeable facts were rechecked immediately before publication
- [ ] Commands, tests, examples, dates, versions, counts and states are correct where used
- [ ] Fact, interpretation and future intent are clearly separated
- [ ] Limits and uncertainty are stated plainly
- [ ] Natural language review removed common generated writing patterns, including em dashes and dash led fragments
- [ ] Independent editorial and factual review completed
- [ ] Consequential claims have accountable human approval
- [ ] GitHub is supporting evidence rather than the whole CodeVolt story
- [ ] Approval obtained if the subject is customer-derived, security-related or otherwise uncertain
