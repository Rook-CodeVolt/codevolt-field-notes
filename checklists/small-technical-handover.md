# Small technical handover

A contained job still needs enough information for another person to understand and reverse it.

## Required record

### Scope

- What problem or request was handled?
- What was deliberately outside scope?

### Change

- Which files, settings or public artifacts changed?
- Why was this the smallest appropriate change?

### Verification

- What exact command, observation or user path was exercised?
- What result was observed?
- What could not be checked?

### Recovery

- How can the change be reversed?
- Does reversal have any dependency, ordering or data consequence?

### Follow-up

- Is anything unresolved?
- Who owns it, or why is no follow-up required?

Do not copy credentials, customer data, private endpoints or raw sensitive logs into the handover.
